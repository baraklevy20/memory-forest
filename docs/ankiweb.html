A forest and a study heatmap that grow from your Anki study history. Every day you learn new cards plants a tree below your deck list, and the heatmap under it shows the days you studied, your streak and the reviews ahead. There is nothing to click and nothing to manage: just study, and watch it grow. To install: in Anki, Tools › Add-ons › Get Add-ons, and paste the code 1255432496.
 
How the forest grows
After a week, a month, four months and four years.
 
A tree starts as a seedling and grows as you come to know its day's cards: within a few weeks it is a young tree, and the days you know best keep growing for years, into ancient giants. If you start forgetting a day's cards, its tree gets a few yellow leaves, and they turn green again once you relearn them. Trees never die. Days when you only review don't plant a tree, but they keep the ones you have healthy. So the forest is a picture of what you know: its size is how much you have learned, and its colour is how well you remember it. Point at a tree to see its day and how well you remember it. Take a week off and a pond appears where the missing days would be. Keep studying and animals move in, and after a year a little cabin appears at the edge of the woods.
 
Your study heatmap Your study days, streaks and upcoming reviews, in your scenery's colours. Hover for details; click a day to open its cards.
  • Views: a rolling timeline, a full year, or your whole streak at once.
  • Select days: Shift-click or Ctrl/Cmd-click days to light up their trees, then click one to open all their cards.
  • Reviews, time or pace: shade days by review count, study time or seconds per answer.
  • Per deck: each deck has its own heatmap and streak.
  • Fair streaks: days with nothing due don't break your streak.
  • Rest days: choose a 5- or 6-day study week.
  • Trophies: medals for your three longest streaks, and a flag for your next goal.
  • Milestones: see when each animal joined your forest.
  • Important days: right-click to mark exams, trips and other dates.
  • Week start: Monday, Sunday, Saturday or your system's setting.
  • Your look: customize its colours and style in the Heatmap tab.

How you study The forest notices how you study, not just what you learn. Good days leave something behind for you to find. Whether bad habits leave a mark too is up to you: pick a Nature on the Forest tab of the settings.
  • Peaceful (the default): only the good things. Days away cost you nothing.
  • Wild: bad habits leave marks until you fix them. Miss two days in a row and smoke rises from the forest as a warning; miss a third and a fire breaks out, spreading while you stay away. A weekend off is fine. Seven days of study put it out.
  • Merciless: a single day without reviews brings down an asteroid on the whole forest, and a new one grows from there. Bad habits leave their marks too.
Change your mind at any time: switch back and your forest is just as it was.
 
Layout
Choose where the forest and the heatmap sit on the Forest tab of the settings.
 
On your phone
Turn on "Show my forest on my phone" on the Forest tab. After your next sync, a Memory Forest deck appears in AnkiDroid: open it to see your forest, with your study heatmap under it. Your computer draws this copy, so it updates whenever Anki syncs there.
 
Compatibility and privacy
  • Anki 2.1.45 or later, on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux). AnkiDroid can show a copy of your forest that your computer sends it (see On your phone).
  • Using Onigiri? It hides the forest until you add it to its grid: here's how.
  • Everything is computed on your computer from your own review history. The only thing that leaves it is your city's name, and only if you turn on the real weather: it is sent to Photon (OpenStreetMap, free, no account) to find the place, and its coordinates to MET Norway (free, no account) for the forecast. The copy of your forest for your phone goes through your own AnkiWeb sync, like any card.
What's new
1.7.0
New
  • Each Nature level can be customized: turn crows, tall grass or tumbleweeds on or off, and choose how many missed days bring the asteroid or a fire (Forest tab, Nature).
Improved
  • When the asteroid waits more than a day, it comes closer each day you miss.
  • The tree under the pointer is now outlined.
  • With the Background layout, the heatmap can stand beside your decks instead of under them (Forest tab, Layout).
Fixed
  • Pointing at a tree no longer sometimes picks the one next to it.
1.6.0
New
  • A new layout, Background: the forest fills the window behind your decks, deck screens and finished decks too (Forest tab, Layout).
  • With Onigiri, the heatmap can have a tile of its own: add Memory Forest Heatmap beside Memory Forest.
Improved
  • Forests with lots of short breaks are no longer crowded with ponds: at most one for every 20 trees.
  • Keeping your earlier trees now keeps everything they brought, animals too.
  • Young trees now grow over their first weeks, as you review their cards.
  • A new forest's first week is easier to follow: the day's new tree glows, and the first messages say how it grows.
  • Ponds look more like water at golden hour.
  • The weather line shows your city as you typed it, with its local name in brackets.
Fixed
  • The heatmap's words stay readable on a background picture, or on an Onigiri page that doesn't match Anki's mode.
  • Right-to-left fixes in the settings for Hebrew and Arabic.
  • Filtered decks: previewing a card no longer plants a tree, and new cards in a filter that doesn't reschedule no longer show as overdue.
  • Clicking a heatmap day no longer sometimes shows an error when the browser opens.
  • Crows and robins on far trees fade into the fog with their tree instead of floating in front of it.
  • Trees by a river no longer shift a little as they grow.
